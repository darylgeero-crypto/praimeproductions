Files used in PrAIme: clean DI samples of a Yamaha RBX bass guitar,
recorded by Andrea Biasior, 24-bit 44.1 kHz mono FLAC. Two
articulations, each with its own bank:
- Finger (samples/finger/, 12 distinct pitches E1-D#2, MIDI 28-39):
  20 chromatic notes MIDI 26-45 (D1-A2), the SFZ's own declared range
  (E covers 26-28, D# covers 39-45), as samples/kits/
  clean-bass-finger-01..20.ogg.
- Pick (samples/pick/, 13 distinct pitches E1-E2, MIDI 28-40):
  21 chromatic notes MIDI 26-46 (D1-A#2), the SFZ's own declared range
  (E covers 26-28, E2 covers 40-46), as samples/kits/
  clean-bass-pick-01..21.ogg.
Each note is derived from the nearest sampled pitch (max 6 semitones
pitch-shift on the top notes, exactly the SFZ's own stretch, done
locally with ffmpeg asetrate; most notes are unshifted). Converted
locally to 44.1 kHz mono OGG (q5), trimmed to 4.3 s with a 0.15 s
fade-out, level-matched to the existing PrAIme banks (peaks -18.4
to -18.6 dB). Pitches verified by FFT after conversion (fundamentals
present at the expected frequencies; the lowest D1 notes show their
2nd/3rd harmonics strongest, as in the raw source files themselves —
the recording's own timbre, not a mapping error).

Clean electric bass is still short of the 50+ bar per bank (finger 20,
pick 21; 41 bass sounds total) — do NOT report it as "done" at 50.
A 50+ single bank is not honest from this source alone: it records
only 12-13 pitches in one low octave, and stretching one sample
across 50 semitones would stop sounding like a bass.

Use in tracks; do not resell the raw samples as a kit.
