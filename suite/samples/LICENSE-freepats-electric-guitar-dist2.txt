Files used in PrAIme: bridge-pickup distorted samples from the main
SFZ mapping "EGuitarFSBS-dist2 bridge 20220911.sfz" (120 regions, 19
distinct sampled pitches, MIDI 35-86 — the SFZ is identical to
dist1's except ampeg_release 0.4 vs 0.5). The README states the bank
was made "by direct sampling a Fender electric guitar (basic model)
using an audio interface with dedicated instrument inputs" and the
samples were "processed through an amplifier and effects rack to
create a distorted, overdriven, and powerful guitar sound". 52
chromatic notes MIDI 35-86 (B1-D6), one per semitone, each derived
from the nearest sampled pitch in the SFZ zones (max 2 semitones
pitch-shift, done locally with ffmpeg asetrate; MIDI 72 IS sampled
in this SFZ — C5_s6_01.flac, key=72 group — so it uses its own
sample at 0 semitones). Converted locally to 44.1 kHz mono OGG
(samples/kits/dist2-guitar-01..52.ogg), trimmed to 4.3 s with a
0.15 s fade-out, level-matched to the existing PrAIme banks (all 19
source samples peak at 0.0 dB, so a uniform -18.5 dB gain). Pitches
verified by FFT after conversion (strongest spectral peak is an
integer harmonic of the expected fundamental, within 25 cents, on
all 52 notes). Tone genuinely different from dist1, measured:
spectral centroid mean 3419 Hz (range 2463-4268 Hz) vs dist1 mean
2380 Hz (range 1719-2905 Hz); dist2 is brighter on all 52 notes
(mean per-note ratio 1.44), confirming a distinct second distorted
profile rather than a near-identical clone. Notes 50-52 are
2.93-3.29 s, not 4.3 s, because their source sample (C#6) is only
3.10 s long; notes 43-44 are 4.02-4.26 s because the F5 source is
4.26 s; no audible content is truncated. SFZ quirk noted: the
hard-velocity D5 group's 4th region references D5_s6_soft_01.flac
instead of a D5_s6_04.flac (which does not exist in the SFZ); only
the _01 take is used, so this does not affect the bank.
