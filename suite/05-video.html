Video generator — real canvas recording

This is not AI video generation from a model, and it does not fake a render. It builds a visualizer video from your song, title and style, and records it in real time — MP4 where your browser supports it, WebM otherwise. For the full video page, use the separate Video Render page already built.

⚡ Near-zero latency graphics: the visuals read your song straight from the browser's AnalyserNode every animation frame — no upload, no server round-trip — so bars, rings and bursts react within a frame of the sound. Smoothing is set low (0.12) on purpose, so it does not lag behind the beat. Not literally zero milliseconds — nothing on a screen is — but frame-level, on-device.

Unique background ready — not used before, and once a video is recorded with it, it will never come back.

Honest timing: this is NOT AI forced-alignment and it does not hear the words — syllables are split by a vowel-group heuristic and spread evenly across the song's length, so the current syllable lights up in yellow as the song plays. Best for a steady flow; it will drift on songs with long pauses or ad-libs.

Subtitles are generated from the lyrics above + the loaded song's length. Load a song first, then download the .srt or .vtt to use with the video in any player / editor. Timing is the same even-distribution estimate as the on-canvas syllables — not AI alignment — so check it against the song and edit the file if a line lands early or late.

Real AI image, not a canvas pattern: generated via Pollinations (free, no key, rate-limited — about 1 image per 45 seconds anonymously, may return 402/503 when busy). The image is drawn behind the video visuals. It is unique by seed, and once it is recorded or sold, it is gone. Check Pollinations' terms before selling a generated image commercially.
