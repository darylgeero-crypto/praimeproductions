AUTHENTICITY — multi-sampled DX7 electric piano, not a runtime oscillator
- The README states the samples were "Recorded from Hexter software
  synthesizer. Hexter is a free software program that accurately
  models the sound generation algorithm of the original instrument."
  So the source is a recording of a DX7 FM electric piano patch
  (the "E. Piano 1" patch) rendered through the Hexter model — a real
  electronic instrument sound, captured as audio files, NOT a Web
  Audio oscillator generated at runtime in the app. The bank is
  labelled "FM Piano" honestly; it is not presented as an acoustic
  piano. Electric piano and electronic instruments are explicitly in
  the wanted list (keys / electronic), and this is the FreePats
  electric piano. The standing exclusion of the FreePats synth-*
  banks (synth-strings/brass/bass/pad) does not apply here: those are
  synth imitations of acoustic instruments, while the DX7 E. Piano 1
  patch IS the electric piano itself — there is no acoustic original
  being faked.
- All 12 v80 source FLACs used are 44.1 kHz mono; durations 7.23-24.88 s
  (long natural FM sustain, low notes ring longest).
- Pitch probe (measure_fmpiano.py): all 12 v80 notes within -1..+2
  cents of keycenter (mean +0.3) — exact Hexter/DX7-model tuning, no
  correction applied (SFZ has no tune= opcodes). Sources peak at
  -0.05 dBFS, uniform across all 12.

SFZ FACTS (read directly from "FM-Piano1 20190916.sfz")
- 3 globals (ampeg_release=0.4): v60 hivel=69, v80 lovel=70 hivel=109,
  v100 lovel=110. 3 groups, 36 regions total (12 per velocity layer).
  No tune= opcodes anywhere.
- Keycenters / lokey-hikey (same in all three layers): F#1 30 (27-32),
  C2 36 (33-38), F#2 42 (39-44), C3 48 (45-50), F#3 54 (51-56),
  C4 60 (57-62), F#4 66 (63-68), C5 72 (69-74), F#5 78 (75-80),
  C6 84 (81-86), F#6 90 (87-92), C7 96 (93-98).
- The SFZ stretches each sample +/-3 semitones around its keycenter
  (alternating C and F# every 6 semitones), but the bank does NOT
  pitch-shift-fill missing semitones (same rule as the
  hang/bagpipe/tubular-bells/old-piano runs).
- NOT used, and why: the v60 and v100 layers are alternate velocity
  takes of the same 12 notes, not separate notes — only the middle
  v80 layer (velocity 70-109) was used, one file per note, same
  convention as the other banks (cf. unused _02 takes in the
  tubular-bells run). This is the library's first bank with real
  velocity layers available; the v60/v100 files were not downloaded
  into the bank, but they exist in the repo if a multi-velocity bank
  is ever wanted.
