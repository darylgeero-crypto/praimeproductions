University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples (MIS)
Source: http://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
Created by Lawrence Fritts, Director of the Electronic Music Studios,
Professor of Composition at the University of Iowa.

Licence statement from the source site: "Since 1997, these recordings
have been freely available on this website and may be downloaded and
used for any projects, without restrictions."

Files used in PrAIme: Eb Alto Saxophone, vibrato, ff dynamic, 32 notes
Db3-Ab5, from the post-2012 single-note files
("sound files/MIS Pitches - 2014/Woodwinds/Eb Alto Saxophone/").
Converted locally to 44.1 kHz mono OGG (samples/kits/alto-sax-01..32.ogg),
level-matched to the existing PrAIme banks (peak about -18 dB).

Use in tracks; do not resell the raw samples as a kit.
