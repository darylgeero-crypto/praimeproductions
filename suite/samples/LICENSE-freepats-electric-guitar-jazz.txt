Files used in PrAIme: bridge-pickup jazz samples from the main SFZ
mapping "EGuitarFSBS-jazz bridge 20260807.sfz" (120 regions, 19
distinct sampled pitches, MIDI 35-86 — the SFZ group layout is
identical to dist1's/dist2's, including the C5 key=72 group and
ampeg_release 0.4). The README states the bank was made "by direct
sampling a Fender electric guitar (basic model) using an audio
interface with dedicated instrument inputs" and the samples were
"processed through an amplifier and effects rack to create a
'jazz' guitar variant". 52 chromatic notes MIDI 35-86 (B1-D6), one
per semitone, each derived from the nearest sampled pitch in the SFZ
zones (max 2 semitones pitch-shift, done locally with ffmpeg
asetrate; MIDI 72 IS sampled in this SFZ — C5_s6_01.flac, key=72
group — so it uses its own sample at 0 semitones). Converted
locally to 44.1 kHz mono OGG (samples/kits/jazz-guitar-01..52.ogg),
trimmed to 4.3 s with a 0.15 s fade-out, level-matched to the
existing PrAIme banks (all 19 source samples peak at 0.0 dB, so a
uniform -18.5 dB gain). Pitches verified by FFT after conversion
(strongest spectral peak is an integer harmonic of the expected
fundamental, within 25 cents, on all 52 notes). Tone genuinely
different from the distorted variants, measured: spectral centroid
mean 1608 Hz (range 442-2496 Hz) vs dist1 mean 2380 Hz (range
1719-2905 Hz) and dist2 mean 3419 Hz (range 2463-4268 Hz); jazz is
darker than dist1 on all 52 notes (mean per-note ratio 0.65) and
darker than dist2 on all 52 notes (mean per-note ratio 0.45),
confirming a distinct clean, warm, mellow jazz profile rather than
a near-identical clone. Notes 50-52 are 2.91-3.27 s, not 4.3 s,
because their source sample (C#6) is only 3.08 s long; notes 43-44
are 3.83-4.06 s because the F5 source is 4.06 s; no audible content
is truncated. SFZ quirk noted: the hard-velocity D5 group's 4th
region references D5_s6_soft_01.flac instead of a D5_s6_04.flac
(which does not exist in the SFZ); only the _01 take is used, so
this does not affect the bank — same quirk as dist1/dist2.
