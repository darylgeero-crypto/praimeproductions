Files used in PrAIme: solo sustain (looped, forte) samples from
Programs/1-solo-mono.sfz -> Programs/legato/dynfade_legato_first_map.sfz,
33 chromatic keys MIDI 36-68 (concert C2-Ab4). The SFZ maps one sample
per key except MIDI 36 and 38, which share d1_looped_f.wav (pitch
keycenter 38); the MIDI 36 file was pitch-shifted down 2 semitones
locally so its sounding pitch is C2.
Converted locally to 44.1 kHz mono OGG
(samples/kits/bari-sax-01..33.ogg), level-matched to the existing
PrAIme banks (peak about -18 dB). Pitches verified by FFT after
conversion (C2 65.4 Hz through Ab4 415.3 Hz).

Use in tracks; do not resell the raw samples as a kit.
