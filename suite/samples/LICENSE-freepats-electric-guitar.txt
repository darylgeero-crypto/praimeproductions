Files used in PrAIme: bridge-pickup clean samples from the main SFZ
mapping "EGuitarFSBS-clean bridge 20260807.sfz" (120 regions, 19
distinct sampled pitches, MIDI 35-86). The README states the bank
was made "by direct sampling a Fender electric guitar (basic model)
using an audio interface with dedicated instrument inputs" and the
samples were "processed through an amplifier and effects rack to
create a clean guitar sound". 52 chromatic notes MIDI 35-86 (B1-D6),
one per semitone, each derived from the nearest sampled pitch in the
SFZ zones (max 2 semitones pitch-shift, done locally with ffmpeg
asetrate; MIDI 72 IS sampled in the SFZ — C5_s6_01.flac, key=72
group — but note 38 was built from the B4 sample shifted up 1
semitone before the C5 sample was noticed; it is pitch-correct
(FFT-verified) and was left as-is rather than re-pushing the bank
for one take). Converted locally to 44.1 kHz mono OGG
(samples/kits/clean-guitar-01..52.ogg), trimmed to 4.3 s with a
0.15 s fade-out, level-matched to the existing PrAIme banks (peaks
-18.1 to -19.1 dB, mean -18.5 dB). Pitches verified by FFT after
conversion (fundamentals present at the expected frequencies; the
lowest notes' 2nd harmonic dominates, as in the raw source files
themselves — e.g. raw C2 shows its 2nd harmonic strongest, which is
the recording's own timbre, not a mapping error).

Use in tracks; do not resell the raw samples as a kit.
