AUTHENTICITY — real glasses of water, not a runtime oscillator
- The source is a multi-sampled recording of real glasses of water
  filled to tune into chromatic notes — captured as audio files,
  NOT a Web Audio oscillator generated at runtime in the app.
- This is a NEW instrument for the library (chromatic percussion,
  high register B4-F6); there is no existing glass/water-glass bank
  to duplicate. Same precedent as the other single-instrument FreePats
  banks (hang, bagpipe, tubular bells, ocarina).
- All 152 source FLACs are 48 kHz MONO; the 20 used take-_01 files
  run 1.32-2.50 s (low notes ring longest, top notes decay fastest).
  No downmix was needed (already mono).
- Pitch probe (measure_glass.py, settled 0.5 s window, harmonic-sum
  FFT + per-harmonic peak report): every take-_01 fundamental sits
  within -5.1..+3.4 cents of its keycenter (mean -0.1) — the glasses
  are in tune at A440. The tones are near-pure sines at the
  fundamental: h1 is dominant by 36+ dB over every other partial
  (h2/h3 sit 50-115 dB below h1, i.e. at the noise floor), so they
  are NOT inharmonic like tubular bells — the harmonic-sum scorer
  gave artefacts on these near-pure tones (note 1: hsum +23 vs h1
  -2.1 cents; C6: -9 vs +1.7; D6: +10 vs +3.4), and the h1 peak is
  the trustworthy reading. Sources peak at about 0.00 dBFS (hot,
  normalised).

SFZ FACTS (read directly from "Glass-20191227.sfz")
- 1 global (ampeg_release=2.0), 20 groups, 152 regions total.
  RandomRegion round-robin takes via seq_position/seq_length
  (5-9 takes per pitch). No velocity layers (no lovel/hivel).
- One tune= opcode: tune=-21 on the first group only (A#4-B4,
  keycenter 70, lokey 69-70). One volume= opcode: volume=-12 on
  the G#5 group (key 80).
- Keycenters (20, the notes used): A#4 70, B4 71, C5 72, C#5 73,
  D5 74, D#5 75, E5 76, F5 77, F#5 78, G5 79, G#5 80, A5 81,
  A#5 82, B5 83, C6 84, C#6 85, D6 86, D#6 87, E6 88, F6 89
  (MIDI 70-89, A#4-F6).
- Takes per pitch: A#4-B4 5, B4 6, C5 6, C#5 7, D5 7, D#5 6,
  E5 9, F5 9, F#5 8, G5 9, G#5 9, A5 8, A#5 7, B5 8, C6 8,
  C#6 8, D6 8, D#6 8, E6 7, F6 9 (152 total).
- The SFZ stretches 2 samples over neighbouring keys (A#4-B4
  covers lokey 69-70, F6 covers lokey 89-93), but the bank does
  NOT pitch-shift-fill missing semitones (same rule as the
  hang/bagpipe/tubular-bells/old-piano/fm-piano1/fm-piano2/
  upright-piano-KW/spanish-classical-guitar runs). The bank uses
  only the 20 real sampled keycenters.
- TUNE DECISION: the SFZ tune=-21 on the first group is NOT
  applied. The probe proves the raw A#4-B4_01 sample already sits
  at A#4 -2.1 cents (h1 peak 465.6 Hz vs 466.16 Hz), so applying
  -21 cents would mistune it to -23 cents. Bagpipe rule: probe
  first — tune is applied only when the probe proves it is a real
  correction (bagpipe sources sat -18..-62 cents flat, tracking
  their tune values); accordion precedent: sources on keycenter
  -> tune not applied. The G#5 volume=-12 needs no handling:
  peak normalisation to -18.5 dBFS equalises it like every note.
