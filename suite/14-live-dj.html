DJ rules — phrase, bass, filter, echo, key

From how popular DJs actually mix: 1 phrase = 8 bars = 32 beats. Drop on the "1", match like-with-like, keep ONE bassline at full weight, FX only at a phrase, never mix over the outgoing drop or vocal hook. Sync matches tempo/phase — it does not pick tracks, align phrases, or manage EQ.

Phrase: — · bar — / 8 · next boundary in — beats
FX: none. Filter centre = neutral.
Deck A: 8A (A minor) · Deck B: 8A (A minor) · Move: same key — safe for a long blend.

Good vs amateur: one locked kick vs trainwreck · sections change together vs phrase-wreck · single bass vs mud · one vocal vs vocal-over-vocal · FX at a phrase vs FX everywhere · loop ≤2 cycles exited on downbeat vs stale loop · spaced peaks vs peaking in the first 10 minutes.
