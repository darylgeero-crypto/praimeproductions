SFZ FACTS (read directly from "Ocarina 20241002.sfz")
- 1 group, 12 regions, loop_mode=loop_continuous with per-region loop
  points; ampeg_release=0.4.
- Pitch keycenters: 66 (F#4), 73 (C#5), 74 (D5), 76 (E5), 77 (F5),
  78 (F#5), 79 (G5), 80 (G#5), 81 (A5), 82 (A#5), 83 (B5), 88 (E6).
- Zones cover MIDI 60-92: 60-70->66, 71-73->73, 74-75->74, 76->76,
  77->77, 78->78, 79->79, 80->80, 81->81, 82->82, 83-85->83, 86-92->88.
  Max pitch shift from a sampled center: 6 semitones (MIDI 60 from 66;
  the SFZ itself maps that zone). No tune= opcodes anywhere in the SFZ.
- The repo also contains samples/A6.flac (5.55 s; probe measured exactly
  1760.00 Hz, a true A6), but it is NOT referenced by any SFZ region, so
  it was NOT used in the bank — the SFZ is the instrument definition and
  its mapped range ends at MIDI 92. The SFZ loop points were not needed:
  the one-shot OGGs keep the full natural sustain length (same as the
  accordion bank).

BUILD RECIPE (build_ocarina.py)
- 33 notes, MIDI 60-92: suite/samples/kits/ocarina-01..33.ogg
  (note01 = MIDI 60 C4 ... note33 = MIDI 92 F#6).
- From the 12 SFZ-mapped FLACs; ffmpeg asetrate pitch-shift per SFZ zone,
  44.1 kHz mono OGG q5. Base rate 48000 (the ukulele-run fix — the
  sources are 48 kHz, so asetrate=48000*2^(semis/12), then
  aresample=44100; using 44100 as the base would repeat the -147-cent
  bug). No trim.
- Level: uniform -18.45 dB cut (sources peak ~-0.05 dBFS) so each built
  file peaks at the library -18.5 dB target.

VERIFICATION (verify_ocarina.py, 2026-11-12)
- ALL 33 files PASS: every built note within 6 cents of its target MIDI
  pitch (worst: C#5-derived notes at +6 cents — the source's own offset,
  preserved by the shift); built peak levels -18.29 to -18.63 dB
  (target -18.5 +/- 1 dB; the small spread is OGG encode loss); all
  decode as vorbis OGG. Built durations 0.74-4.56 s.
- Kits OGG count: 1555 -> 1588.

WIRING
- suite/real-sounds.js: ocarinaBank (33), PraimeSoundLibrary ocarina:33,
  total 1555 -> 1588. NOT wired into SuperBeat melody voices, same as
  the sax/guitar/bass/timpani/ukulele banks — piano/steel/nylon remain
  the only melody voices.
- Ocarina is range-limited by nature (MIDI 60-92, 33 notes): it stays
  under 50 sounds, like harp (23), timpani (25), accordion (17+17) and
  ukulele (29+2). No stretching beyond the SFZ range was done.
