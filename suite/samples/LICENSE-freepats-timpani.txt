SFZ FACTS (read directly from "Timpani 20240809.sfz")
- 12 groups, 24 regions, 2 round-robin takes (seq_position 1/2) per group.
- 11 distinct pitch samples: 1B, 1C, 1D, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B, 3C, 4A, 5A, 5B
  (each as _v5_rr1.flac / _v5_rr2.flac, 44.1 kHz stereo FLAC, 2.26-8.18 s).
- Pitch keycenters: 38, 39, 42, 43, 45, 46, 47, 49, 53, 55, 57.
  Zones cover MIDI 36-60 (C2-C4): 36-38->38, 39-40->39, 41-42->42, 43->43,
  44-45->45, 46->46, 47->47, 48-50->49, 51-54->53, 55->55, 56->57, 57-60->57.
- Max pitch shift from a sampled center: 3 semitones (MIDI 60 from 57;
  the SFZ itself maps 57-60 to the 5B sample). All other notes within 2.
- SFZ quirks: key=55's second round-robin reuses the 4A sample
  (pitch_keycenter 53, tune +20); key=56's first region is the 5B sample
  with pitch_keycenter 57, tune +20, and its second region is the 5A
  sample (pitch_keycenter 55, tune +50). Only the _01 (rr1) take of each
  sample's primary region is used in the PrAIme bank, so these quirks do
  not affect it.
- SFZ sub-semitone tune offsets (max 60 cents: 1C -60, 2A +60, 3A +50,
  3B -50, 3C -30, 4A +20, 5A +50, 5B +20) were NOT applied in the build:
  probe FFT showed every source's harmonic series fits its keycenter
  within a few cents (e.g. 1C's 2.13x/3.12x modes imply f0 77.9 Hz =
  keycenter 39 exactly; the odd 81 Hz peak is an inharmonic partial, not
  the fundamental), so the keycenter is the declared pitch as a sampler
  would play it.

BUILD RECIPE (build_timpani.py)
- 25 notes, MIDI 36-60: suite/samples/kits/timpani-01..25.ogg
  (note01 = MIDI 36 C2 ... note25 = MIDI 60 C4).
- From the 11 rr1 FLACs; ffmpeg asetrate pitch-shift per SFZ zone,
  44.1 kHz mono OGG q5, 4.3 s trim, 0.15 s fade-out.
- Level: per-sample peak measured on first 4.3 s mono, gain set so each
  built file peaks at -18.5 dB. CORRECTION FOUND THIS RUN: measuring peaks
  with ffmpeg -ac 1 (which SUMS stereo channels) reads 3.01 dB hotter
  than the build's pan=mono|0.5*c0+0.5*c1 (which AVERAGES); the first
  build came out at -21.5 dB and was rebuilt with +3.01 dB gain.
  The same probe-then-pan recipe was used for the FSBS guitar banks, so
  those banks are probably also ~3 dB below their intended -18.5 dB
  target (uniformly, so relative balance is unaffected) — noted, not
  rebuilt here; out of scope for this run.
- Notes from the short 5B source (2.26 s) are shorter as pitch rises:
  notes 21-25 are 2.39/2.26/2.13/2.01/1.90 s; note 18 is 4.27 s (4A
  source length). No audible content truncated.

VERIFICATION (verify_timpani.py, 2026-11-12)
- Timpani partials are inharmonic (the ~1.5x mode sits at 1.45-1.57x
  depending on the drum), so a fixed-partial pitch test fails good notes
  (first attempt: 10 of 25 "failed" at up to +104 cents on the naive
  1.5x/2.0x/3.0x model). Correct check: each built note's top 6 spectral
  peaks must match its source sample's top 6 peaks scaled by
  2^(semis/12) within 25 cents. Result: ALL 25 pass (5-6 of 6 peaks
  within 25 cents; worst single weak partial +76 cents on 2B's 4th peak,
  +14 cents on 5B's 5th peak — weak/noisy partials, not the pitch).
- Built peak levels: -18.31 to -18.99 dB (target -18.5 +/- 1 dB). All 25
  decode as vorbis OGG.
