Separate & Re-Beat

1. Load a song or a recorded vocal

Load a stereo song to separate it, or a vocal you already recorded. Separation here is centre extraction, not AI stem separation: the vocal estimate is the centre of the stereo field, the backing is what is left when the centre is removed. Mono files pass through untouched.

2. Analyse it

Measured from the actual audio: peak, RMS, crest, low/mid/high band energy, and a tempo estimate from onset energy. Tempo from a vocal alone, with no drums, is a best guess — you can override it below.

3. Render a new backing beat for it

The new beat is synthesised in the browser at the BPM and in the genre/mood you choose — it is not a sample, and it does not restyle an existing recording. It renders for the full length of your vocal, looping its pattern.

4. Mix vocal + new beat

Honest limits: centre extraction leaves bleed when vocals, kick and bass share the centre. Mood from audio alone is a suggestion from band energy + your lyrics keywords, not a certified mood detector. The beat is a new synth pattern, not a conversion of the old beat.
