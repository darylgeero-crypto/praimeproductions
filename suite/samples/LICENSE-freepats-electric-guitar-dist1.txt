Files used in PrAIme: bridge-pickup distorted samples from the main
SFZ mapping "EGuitarFSBS-dist1 bridge 20220911.sfz" (120 regions, 18
distinct sampled pitches, MIDI 35-86). The README states the bank
was made "by direct sampling a Fender electric guitar (basic model)
using an audio interface with dedicated instrument inputs" and the
samples were "processed through an amplifier and effects rack to
create a distorted, overdriven, and powerful guitar sound". 52
chromatic notes MIDI 35-86 (B1-D6), one per semitone, each derived
from the nearest sampled pitch in the SFZ zones (max 2 semitones
pitch-shift, done locally with ffmpeg asetrate; MIDI 72 is unmapped
in the SFZ, so it uses the B4 sample shifted up 1 semitone, as in
the clean bank). Converted locally to 44.1 kHz mono OGG
(samples/kits/dist-guitar-01..52.ogg), trimmed to 4.3 s with a
0.15 s fade-out, level-matched to the existing PrAIme banks (peaks
-18.1 to -19.1 dB, mean -18.6 dB). Pitches verified by FFT after
conversion (strongest spectral peak is an exact integer harmonic of
the expected fundamental, within 11 cents, on all 52 notes, with the
fundamental itself carrying 17-100% of peak energy; the harmonic
dominance is the distortion's own timbre, not a mapping error).
Spectral centroid 1935-2588 Hz vs 649-2055 Hz for the clean bank at
the same notes, confirming a genuinely distorted tone. The top note
(dist-guitar-52, MIDI 86) is 4.18 s, not 4.3 s, because its source
sample is only 4.43 s long and decays to near-silence by 4.0 s; no
audible content is truncated.

Use in tracks; do not resell the raw samples as a kit.
