AUTHENTICITY — real recorded piano, not synth
- All 78 source FLACs in samples/ are 44.1 kHz stereo; durations
  1.13-20.45 s (long natural piano decays on the low notes).
- Pitch probe (measure_oldpiano.py): the piano's own tuning is kept
  as recorded — scattered within +/-27 cents of keycenter, mean
  +1.1 cents. A 2 s-window harmonic check confirmed the outliers are
  real, not measurement noise: the bottom three notes are genuinely
  flat (G#0 -32, A0 -28, A#0 -25 cents; the 0.5 s probe read -47/-57
  on the bottom two because of FFT resolution at ~26 Hz) and B2 is
  ~+26 cents sharp. That uneven tuning IS the honky-tonk character
  of a historic player piano, so NO pitch correction was applied
  (SFZ has no tune= opcodes). Sources peak at -0.21 to -4.08 dBFS.

BUILD RECIPE (build_oldpiano.py)
- 78 notes: suite/samples/kits/old-piano-01..78.ogg (note01 =
  MIDI 20 G#0, note78 = MIDI 107 B7). From the 78 FLACs, decoded to
  44.1 kHz mono (stereo sources downmixed; peak measured on the mono
  WAV so the -18.5 dBFS target applies to the actual output).
  No pitch shift (sources are already 44.1 kHz; no tune= exists to
  apply). 44.1 kHz mono OGG q5. No trim, no fade: long decays
  (up to 20.45 s) are real piano resonance.
- Level: per-file gain so each built file peaks at the library -18.5
  dB target (gains -14.42 to -18.29 dB, sources peak -0.21 to
  -4.08 dBFS). One Vorbis compensation WAS needed: old-piano-72
  (F7, MIDI 101) first decoded at -19.76 dB (encoder undershoot);
  rebuilt with +1.26 dB gain and decoded at -17.89 dB, then rebuilt
  again at -0.61 dB relative to that and decoded at -18.78 dB
  (0.28 dB from target) — kept. Lesson carried forward from the
  hang/tubular-bells runs: ALWAYS verify the DECODED OGG peak (as
  verify_*.py does) and compensate per file; the encoder's peak
  shift is not a fixed offset, so check after each rebuild.
