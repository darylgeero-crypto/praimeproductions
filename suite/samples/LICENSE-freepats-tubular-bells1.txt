AUTHENTICITY — real recorded tubular bells, not synth
- All 19 source FLACs in samples/ are 44.1 kHz mono 24-bit; the 10
  first takes used are 8.66-15.88 s long (long natural bell decays).
- Pitch probe (measure_tubular.py): the bells are INHARMONIC — the
  strongest partial is ~5.35x the fundamental and a harmonic-sum
  scorer is fooled by it (it scored the C4 source at +118 cents).
  The 2x partial sits at 2.00x the keycenter frequency for every
  note (C4 +3.3, D4 +2.0, E4 +3.4, F#4 +3.4, G#4 +5.3, A#4 +7.5,
  C5 +6.7, D5 +6.9, E5 +11.1, A#5 +11.2 cents), so the sources sit on
  their keycenters — the instrument's own tuning, no correction
  applied (SFZ has no tune= opcodes). Sources peak at ~0.00 dBFS.

SFZ FACTS (read directly from "TubularBells 20241130.sfz")
- 1 global (ampeg_release=2), 10 groups, 20 regions. 9 groups have 2
  random takes each (lorand/hirand 0-0.5 / 0.5-1); the A#5 group has
  2 regions pointing at the same single sample (A#5.flac).
- Keycenters / lokey-hikey: C4 60 (57-61), D4 62 (62-63), E4 64
  (64-65), F#4 66 (66-67), G#4 68 (68-69), A#4 70 (70-71), C5 72
  (72-73), D5 74 (74-75), E5 76 (76-79), A#5 82 (80-85).
- Dynamics are in the filenames: C4_f, D4/E4/G#4/A#4/C5/D5/E5_ff,
  F#4_fff, A#5 (no dynamic suffix). No tune= opcodes anywhere.
- A second SFZ, "TubularBells small 20241130.sfz", maps the same
  keys with single takes from samples/small/ — the full SFZ is the
  canonical instrument and the full-size samples were used; the
  small variant was NOT used.
- NOT used, and why: the SFZ maps the gaps between the 10 real notes
  by stretching neighbours (e.g. C4 covers 57-61, E5 covers 76-79,
  A#5 covers 80-85), but the bank does NOT pitch-shift-fill missing
  semitones (same rule as the hang/bagpipe runs). The second takes
  (_02) are random alternates of the same 9 notes, not separate
  notes; only the first take (_01) of each note was used (A#5 has a
  single file). The SFZ is the instrument definition.

BUILD RECIPE (build_tubular.py)
- 10 notes: suite/samples/kits/tubular-bells-01..10.ogg (note01 =
  MIDI 60 C4, 02 = 62 D4, 03 = 64 E4, 04 = 66 F#4, 05 = 68 G#4,
  06 = 70 A#4, 07 = 72 C5, 08 = 74 D5, 09 = 76 E5, 10 = 82 A#5).
- From the 9 _01 FLACs + A#5.flac. No pitch shift (sources are
  already 44.1 kHz, so the -147-cent bug cannot apply; no tune=
  exists to apply). 44.1 kHz mono OGG q5. No trim.
- Level: per-file gain so each built file peaks at the library -18.5
  dB target (gains -18.50/-18.49 dB, A#5 -18.43 dB, sources peak
  ~0.00 dBFS). One Vorbis compensation WAS needed: C5 first decoded
  at -19.51 dB (encoder undershoot, the reverse of the hang bank's
  overshoot) and was rebuilt with +1.01 dB (gain -17.49 dB); it then
  decoded at -18.02 dB. Lesson carried forward from the hang run:
  ALWAYS verify the DECODED OGG peak (as verify_*.py does) and
  compensate per file — tonal bells can undershoot as well as
  overshoot.
